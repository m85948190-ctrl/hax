Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Product: HAxBRO AI / Hacks Bro AI

This policy explains the main data flows users should expect when using HAxBRO AI. The product is an evolving web app; the exact data involved depends on the feature used and the current app version.

1. Information stored in your browser

HAxBRO can store your username, preferences, conversation history, onboarding acknowledgement, and other workspace data in browser storage. When local AI is enabled, model files and runtime caches may also be stored on your device. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to access locally stored data. Clearing browser data may remove it.

2. Local AI model downloads

On supported devices, model files may be downloaded from designated model distribution services and processed in your browser. The first download transfers data over the network. Subsequent visits may reuse cached files if they remain available. Browser-local inference does not mean every HAxBRO feature is offline or that all network traffic stops.

3. Network features and external services

Website hosting, API-backed workspace features, web search, remote content retrieval, and embedded third-party components may communicate with HAxBRO hosting or those providers. Those requests may expose technical information such as IP address, browser details, request metadata, and the content necessary to perform the requested feature. Review the relevant third-party policies when using an external service.

4. Uploaded content and prompts

Some tools may send the data needed to complete a request to HAxBRO API endpoints or external services. Do not assume that a prompt, upload, or generated file stays only on your device unless the specific feature clearly indicates that it is processed locally. Avoid submitting secrets or sensitive personal information unless you understand the feature's data flow.

5. Cookies, storage, and analytics

HAxBRO uses browser storage for product functionality. The site may also load analytics, support chat, or other third-party scripts configured in the current version. Those providers may use their own cookies or identifiers under their own policies and browser settings.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable care is taken in product design, but no website or local storage method can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Locally stored data generally remains until you clear it, the browser evicts it, or the app removes it. Server-side data retention depends on the specific feature and service used.

7. Your choices

You can clear HAxBRO site data through your browser settings. Doing so may remove saved conversations, settings, and model caches and may require a new model download. You can decline the onboarding agreement and leave without entering the workspace.

8. Policy updates

This policy may change as features and service integrations change. The effective date above identifies the version shown by the onboarding flow.
